    The Artist

    About Homer T. Jones

    Homer T. Jones believes the most meaningful drawings are often the simplest. With a steady hand and a few confident lines, Homer captures the places and faces that matter most: the front porch of a first home, a faithful companion, the chapel where two lives became one.

    Every commission is drawn by hand, then thoughtfully set onto postcards, porcelain plates, and keepsakes designed to be given, displayed, and treasured for years to come.

    “A good sketch doesn’t record every detail. It remembers the feeling.”

    Questions

    Before you commission

    What should I send as a reference?

    A clear, well-lit photograph is ideal. Straight-on photos work beautifully for homes and buildings; for pets and people, a photo taken at eye level in natural light captures the most character. Attach one photo with your request, and we’ll follow up by email if more would help.

    How much does a commission cost?

    Every piece is quoted individually based on the item, quantity, and detail of the sketch. You’ll receive a personal quote after submitting your request. There’s no obligation, and nothing is charged until you approve.

    How long will it take?

    Timing depends on the item and quantity, and your quote will include an estimated completion date. If you have an event or deadline, note it on your request and we’ll let you know right away whether it’s possible.

    Will I see the sketch before it’s produced?

    Yes. You’ll receive a digital proof of your sketch to review, and small refinements are welcome before anything goes into production.

    Can you handle larger orders?

    Gladly. Postcards and notecards are well suited to weddings, events, closing gifts, and businesses. Include your quantity on the request and we’ll tailor a quote.

    How do I pay?

    Once you approve your quote, you’ll receive an invoice by email. Work begins as soon as it’s settled.
