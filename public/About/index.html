About the Art

Meet the Artist

Every piece begins with a single ink line. Working from a favorite photograph, each sketch is drawn by hand with a light, confident touch, keeping only the lines that matter: the pitch of a roof, the curve of a doorway, the welcome of a front porch.

The style is quiet and classic, in the spirit of the old picture postcard: clean pen work, generous white space, and just enough detail to let a place feel like home. Each finished drawing is then set onto postcards, porcelain plates, and keepsakes made to be given, displayed, and kept.

“A good sketch doesn’t record every detail. It remembers the feeling.”

The Process

A personal, unhurried process

Every piece is handled one at a time, from the first email to the finished keepsake.

  1. I.

    Send a note

    Email a favorite photograph and a few words about the piece you have in mind.

  2. II.

    Receive your quote

    Each request is reviewed personally and answered with pricing and an estimated timeline.

  3. III.

    Approve the sketch

    Your sketch is drawn by hand. You review a proof and request refinements before anything is made.

  4. IV.

    Treasure it

    Your finished keepsake is carefully produced, wrapped, and sent on its way.

Questions

Good to know

How do I request a piece?

Simply email homert.jonescards@gmail.com with your photograph and a few details, or send a note from the Contact page. There’s no online checkout; every piece is arranged personally.

What should I send as a reference?

A clear, well-lit photograph is ideal. Straight-on photos work beautifully for homes and buildings; for pets and people, a photo taken at eye level in natural light captures the most character. Attach it to your email, and we’ll follow up if more would help.

How much does a piece cost?

Every piece is quoted individually based on the item, quantity, and detail of the sketch. Email your idea and you’ll receive a personal quote, with no obligation.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on the item and quantity, and your quote will include an estimated completion date. If you have an event or deadline, mention it in your email and we’ll let you know right away whether it’s possible.

Will I see the sketch before it’s produced?

Yes. You’ll receive a digital proof of your sketch to review, and small refinements are welcome before anything goes into production.

Can you handle larger orders?

Gladly. Postcards and notecards are well suited to weddings, events, closing gifts, and businesses. Include your quantity in your email and we’ll tailor a quote.
